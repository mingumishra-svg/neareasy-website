NearEasy - Account Deletion

We're sorry to see you go. This page explains how to delete your NearEasy account and what data will be removed.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly from the NearEasy app:

1
Open NearEasy app

Launch the NearEasy app on your phone

2
Go to Settings

Tap on the Settings icon (gear icon) in the app

3
Tap "Delete Account"

Scroll down and tap on the "Delete Account" button

4
Confirm Deletion

Confirm your choice when prompted. Your account will be permanently deleted.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

The following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your account profile (name, email, phone number)
  • Your authentication credentials
  • Your call history logs stored in our database
  • Any preferences or settings associated with your account

Important Notice

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
  • You will need to create a new account if you wish to use NearEasy again
  • Deletion is processed immediately

Data Retention

After account deletion:

  • Immediately deleted: Profile data, authentication info, call logs
  • Anonymous analytics: Previously collected anonymous usage data may be retained for service improvement but cannot be linked to you
